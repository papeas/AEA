Our Services

A comprehensive range of quality professional services, tailored to businesses of every size — from the sole entrepreneur to large multinational groups.

Audit & Assurance Services

We provide statutory audit of annual and interim financial statements in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) and International Standards on Auditing (ISA), giving stakeholders confidence in your reported results.

  • Statutory audit of annual financial statements
  • Audit of interim financial statements
  • Reporting under IFRS
  • Engagements conducted under ISA
  • Assurance reporting for stakeholders and regulators
  • Practical recommendations arising from the audit

Tax Services

Our corporate and personal tax services help you remain compliant while structuring your affairs efficiently under the Cyprus tax regime.

  • Registration of companies with the Inland Revenue
  • Preparation and filing of corporate tax returns
  • Preparation and filing of personal tax returns
  • Tax planning and structuring
  • Appeals against tax assessments
  • Ongoing liaison with the tax authorities

Compliance Services

We take the administrative burden off your shoulders by performing complete payroll and statutory compliance functions for companies and individuals.

  • Full payroll function for companies and individuals
  • Registration with the Social Insurance Department
  • Administration of payments to Income Tax
  • Administration of payments to Social Insurance
  • Preparation of employer returns
  • Ongoing statutory compliance support

Advisory Services

Whether you are assessing a new venture or an acquisition, our advisory team delivers the analysis you need to make informed decisions with confidence.

  • Preparation of business plans
  • Feasibility studies for new ventures and opportunities
  • Financial due diligence reports
  • Valuation reports for companies
  • Transaction support
  • Ad-hoc financial analysis and advice

Insolvency Services

We support companies and their stakeholders through periods of financial difficulty, acting with fairness, transparency and professionalism throughout the process.

  • Acting as liquidator of companies
  • Acting as receiver of companies in financial difficulty
  • Members’ voluntary liquidations
  • Creditors’ interests representation
  • Orderly realisation and distribution of assets
  • Statutory reporting throughout the appointment

Fiduciary Services

We help local and international clients establish and maintain their presence in Cyprus with a full suite of corporate and fiduciary services.

  • Formation of Cyprus companies
  • Provision of registered office address
  • Provision of trustee services
  • Corporate administration and secretarial support
  • Maintenance of statutory records
  • Ongoing corporate compliance
