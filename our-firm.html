Company Profile

Who we are

Ekkeshis + Ierodiakonou is an independent provider of a comprehensive range of quality professional services in the fields of Compliance, Audit, Tax, Insolvency and Advisory.

We serve a wide range of businesses — from the sole entrepreneur and the owner-managed business to large multinational groups. The firm comprises a team of qualified, high-calibre individuals, members of leading professional bodies of accountants, with years of experience in the fields of compliance, audit, tax and advisory services.

  • Independent — objective advice, free of conflicts
  • Qualified — members of leading professional bodies of accountants
  • Central — located in the heart of Nicosia, Cyprus
“Our mission is to continuously add value to our clients’ businesses through the provision of the highest quality professional services on a timely, efficient and cost-effective basis.” Our Mission
Our People

Meet the Partners

At Ekkeshis + Ierodiakonou we believe our people are our most important asset. Our team consists of highly qualified individuals with considerable practical experience in their field of expertise.
