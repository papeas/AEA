# Nexus — Service Plans & Limits

Three packages. Every project gets an **exact quote up front** — the figures below are starting points, not fixed prices. When unsure which fits, ask about the client's business and recommend one.

> ⚠️ **Confirm these limits.** The prices and feature lists come from the live Plans section and are accurate. The **limits / "not included"** lines marked _(confirm)_ are sensible defaults I drafted — Andreas should review and correct them, then remove this note.

---

## Basic — from €400
**"Get online, simply."** For a business that just needs a clean, credible presence.

**Included**
- One-page website
- Mobile-friendly & fast
- Instagram & contact links
- Basic Google / SEO setup
- Live in about a week

**Limits — not included** _(confirm)_
- Single page only — no extra/inner pages
- One language
- Contact **links** only (no contact or booking forms)
- No e-commerce / online payments
- No custom integrations or CMS/admin editing
- ~1 round of revisions

---

## Premium — €600–850  ·  *Most popular*
**"A complete site, built around your brand."** A full multi-page site for an established business.

**Included**
- Up to 5 pages, custom design
- English & Greek
- Contact & booking forms
- Google Maps & SEO setup
- Customer reviews section
- 1 month of support included

**Limits — not included** _(confirm)_
- Up to **5 pages** — additional pages quoted separately
- Two languages (English & Greek)
- No online store / card payments
- Standard integrations only (forms, Maps, socials)
- ~2 rounds of revisions
- Support included for 1 month after launch, then optional

---

## Ultra — Let's talk (custom quote)
**"Online shop or something bespoke."** E-commerce or custom-built projects, scoped per client.

**Included**
- E-commerce with card payments
- Product setup & training
- Customer reviews system
- English & Greek
- Custom features on request
- 3 months of priority support

**Limits — not included** _(confirm)_
- Scope and price set per project via a custom quote
- Third-party costs billed separately: payment-processor fees, premium plugins/apps, domain, and any paid hosting/add-ons
- Timeline depends on scope (agreed before work starts)
